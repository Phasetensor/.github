# Code of conduct

PhaseTensor welcomes respectful, evidence-based scientific and engineering discussion. Treat participants fairly, critique ideas rather than individuals, and acknowledge contributions and prior work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, disclosure of private information without consent, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when necessary, taking confidentiality and proportionality into account.
