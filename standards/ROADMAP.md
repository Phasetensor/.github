# Research roadmap

## Active priority

ShotWeave — constrained quantum measurement allocation. Initial benchmark: uniform, weighted and adaptive allocation, 10,000 total shots including pilot measurements, at most two device submissions, estimation error, submissions and elapsed time.

## Planned

BornLens — statistical comparison of observables with a predefined tolerance and three possible outcomes: within tolerance, beyond tolerance, or insufficient evidence.

## Candidates

CalibMap, VintageCheck, CurveDiff, LayoutCheck, OrbitalBudget, SurfaceCheck, TensorBudget and StencilLab.

Candidate names do not imply implementation, availability, or validated novelty. Each requires a problem statement, user and baseline analysis, and measurable validation criteria before development.
